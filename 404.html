THE MAIN VENUE

Jinniu Hotel Chengdu

Located on Jinquan Road, Jinniu District, Chengdu, Jinniu Hotel is situated within the Chengdu West Tourism & Cultural Zone. Adjacent to Jinniu Corridor Bridge and Yi Garden, the hotel boasts an elegant and tranquil environment alongside comprehensive living and leisure facilities. As a garden villa-style state guest hotel dedicated to receiving foreign heads of state and central state leaders, it covers nearly 600 mu (1 mu ≈ 0.0667 hectares) and boasts a history of over 50 years.
