General Information

Where visual intelligence meets its next frontier.

The 2nd CVL Workshop on New Trends in Computer Vision keeps pace with global frontiers of computer vision and focuses on technological advances and research innovation.

It brings together top scholars and industry practitioners worldwide, including numerous researchers with deep academic connections and cooperative ties to the Computer Vision Lab (CVL), to discuss recent progress, key technical breakthroughs and future directions in computer vision.

Adhering to high-standard and professional academic positioning, the workshop features keynote speeches, invited talks and panel discussions. It explores cutting-edge computer vision research including large models, AIGC, visual perception innovation and cross-modal fusion, and analyzes relevant technological trends and industrial prospects.

THE MAIN VENUE

Jinniu Hotel Chengdu

Nestled on Jinquan Road, Jinniu District, this hotel is located within Chengdu West Tourism & Cultural Zone. It is adjacent to scenic sites including Jinniu Corridor Bridge and Yi Garden, featuring an elegant and serene environment with complete living and leisure supporting facilities.

As a garden villa-style state guest hotel that mainly receives foreign heads of state and national central leaders, Jinniu Hotel covers an area of nearly 600 mu (1 mu ≈ 0.0667 hectares) and has a history of more than 50 years.
